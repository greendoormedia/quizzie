Quizzie is the multiplayer trivia game built for game nights. One device, up to five players — take turns as Quizmaster, answer questions across 8 categories, and see who can outsmart the room. Easy to pick up, impossible to put down.

How to play

Three Steps
to Victory

01
Add Your Players

Enter up to 5 player names and get ready to compete. No accounts, no setup — just names and a game.

02
Pass & Answer

The Quizmaster reads the question. Players answer in turn. Get it right, earn a point. Get it wrong — the next player gets their shot.

03
Crown the Winner

After every round, check the leaderboard. At the end of the game, one player is crowned the ultimate Quizzie champion.
