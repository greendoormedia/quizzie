Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Quizzie app published by Green Door Media Limited.

Information We Collect

Quizzie does not require account registration and does not collect any personal information from users. Player names entered during gameplay are stored locally on your device only and are never transmitted to our servers.

Data Storage

All game data including player names, scores and progress is stored locally on your device. We do not have access to this data.

Third Party Services

Quizzie uses the following third party services:

  • Google Firebase Analytics — used to understand how the app is used. Data collected is anonymous. See Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics — used to identify and fix app crashes. See Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy

Advertising

Quizzie does not currently display advertisements.

Children's Privacy

Quizzie is intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy please contact us at greendoormediasupport@gmail.com
